## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- The scale: a worry is a stone on a brass balance, and weights are laid one by one until the beam tips. You rate the stone's heft before and after.
- The catalogue: 1,209 concrete worries in 24 spheres and 15,900 weights, plus universal and night weights.
  - Eight metals: facts with sources, the worst case step by step, small actions, the other side, questions, voices, feathers, and honest steel for heavy fears.
  - Search understands how people really put it.
- Checking the scales: you set a day to find out, and that morning the app asks «did it happen?». Your own record becomes a weight of its own.
- Shelves by sphere. A stone is polished smoother each time its worry is outweighed, and pebbles can be thrown into the river.
- The well «and then what?», with what holds you up at the bottom.
- The case: kept weights, weights you engrave yourself, and «not for me».
- The hour for worries: shelve a stone until evening. In the morning, a weight of the day.
- From midnight to five the chamber goes dark and has weights of its own. Words about self-harm open a help screen instead of the scale.
- Synthesised sounds for each metal.
- English and Russian, a demo build, and community links to the StillCraft bot.
